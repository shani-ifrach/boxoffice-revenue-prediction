# EDA findings

- Dataset rows analyzed: 3,607.
- Movies with both positive budget and revenue: 2,816.
- Highest median-revenue season: Winter ($34,737,199; n=801).
- Highest median-ROI season: Summer (1.24; n=895).
- Highest median-revenue genre among genres with at least 20 movies: Adventure ($117,209,004; n=684).

These are descriptive associations, not causal effects. Genre and director comparisons should be interpreted with their sample sizes and with the source's popularity-based sampling limitation in mind.
