# EDA findings

- Dataset rows analyzed: 2,144.
- Movies with both positive budget and revenue: 1,881.
- Highest median-revenue season: Spring ($69,034,982; n=460).
- Highest median-ROI season: Summer (1.60; n=547).
- Highest median-revenue genre among genres with at least 20 movies: Adventure ($197,744,825; n=525).

These are descriptive associations, not causal effects. Genre and director comparisons should be interpreted with their sample sizes and with the source's popularity-based sampling limitation in mind.
